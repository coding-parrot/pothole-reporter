Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 21 August 2026

Summary

Pothole Reporter has no project-operated server, advertising network, or app account system. Reports and any recordings you choose to create are kept on your device.

It is not fully offline: selected road images are sent directly to OpenAI for analysis, and precise coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Karnataka GIS services for routing. Opening an email draft hands complaint data and a photo to your email app.

Scope and controller

This policy applies to the Pothole Reporter Android app and its project pages. “The project” below means the Pothole Reporter open-source project identified by that name in its store listing and source repository.

The project does not receive a central copy of your reports because it operates no collection backend. The external services listed below receive data directly from your device for the features you request.

Data the app handles

DataWhat happensPurpose
Road photos and video frames A manual photo, or selected still frames sampled during Drive Mode or from saved drive video, is resized and sent to the OpenAI API. Images are not blurred first and may contain faces, number plates, people, vehicles, or shopfronts. The complete saved video file is not uploaded by the app. Assess visible road damage and create an editable local report.
Precise location and drive motion data The app reads latitude and longitude and may retain a local drive track with accuracy, speed, heading, and timestamps. Coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Karnataka GIS endpoints. Viewing the in-app map requests OpenStreetMap tiles for the displayed area. Find an address, classify the road, identify a supported local body, display report locations, and suppress repeated Drive Mode observations.
OpenAI API key The key you enter is stored in the app's local WebView storage and sent to OpenAI in the authorization header for API requests. It is never built into the app or sent to a project-operated server. Authenticate usage billed to your own OpenAI account.
Road address and procurement candidates For a report that can be routed, the reverse-geocoded road address and a shortlist of public procurement records may be sent to OpenAI for a probable contract match. Offer an optional, reviewable contract reference. A match is not proof of responsibility or warranty.
Name, settings, reports, labels, photos, footage, and drive summaries These are stored on the device. Your name is placed in the complaint signature. Settings include language, model, image detail, recording, frame-saving, and debug choices. Run the app, preserve history, allow review, and prepare complaint drafts.
Email draft and attachment When you choose Email, the recipient, subject, complaint body, and road photo are handed to your email app. The email provider may sync a draft under its own terms. Pothole Reporter does not press Send. Let you review, edit, and choose whether to send a complaint.
Saved or exported evaluation files If you enable frame saving, analysed frames and a model-verdict manifest are written to the device's Documents area. If you export labelled data, a ZIP is handed to Android's share sheet and goes only to the destination you choose. Let you inspect model output and build a human-labelled evaluation set.

External recipients

  • OpenAI: API credentials, selected images, prompts, and—in the contract-matching flow—a road address and procurement shortlist. Requests set store: false, which prevents Responses API application-state retention but does not override OpenAI's security, abuse-monitoring, or legal retention. See OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap services: coordinates sent to Nominatim for reverse geocoding and map-area requests sent to the tile service. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy and Nominatim policy.
  • Karnataka GIS/KSRSAC: coordinates sent to government map endpoints to check municipal, gram-panchayat, and national-highway layers.
  • Your email and sharing providers: data is transferred only when you open a complaint draft or use Android's share sheet. Their own policies and retention rules apply.

Like other internet services, these recipients can receive an IP address and standard connection metadata. They may process data outside your state or country, including outside India. The project does not control their independent retention or legal obligations.

Permissions

  • Camera captures a manual road photo or foreground Drive Mode footage.
  • Precise or approximate location locates and routes a report and records a drive track while Drive Mode is active.
  • Internet reaches the external services described above.

The app does not request Android background-location permission. Drive Mode stops if the app leaves the foreground. Mount the phone before moving and do not interact with it while driving.

Retention and deletion

  • Drive recording is off by default. If you enable it, clips are written to app-private storage on the device.
  • Reports, report photos, drive summaries, and recorded footage remain in app-private storage until the app deletes them automatically or you delete them.
  • With Debug mode off, successfully analysed drive footage is deleted after analysis completes without failed frame requests. Debug mode keeps it. You can also delete footage from History.
  • Delete all app data clears local reports, photos, drive summaries, app-held footage, the API key, sender name, settings, and the app's Documents/pothole-frames folder.
  • Files already exported, shared, attached, copied elsewhere, or sent are outside the app's control and must be deleted from their destination separately.
  • Data already processed by OpenAI, OpenStreetMap services, Karnataka GIS, an email provider, or a complaint recipient is subject to that recipient's retention and deletion process.

The app creates no Pothole Reporter account, so there is no project account to delete. Android backup is disabled for the app.

Security and your choices

Network calls in the current app use HTTPS. Local app data is protected by Android's device and application storage controls, but the app does not add separate at-rest encryption to the OpenAI key stored in WebView storage. Protect your device, use a revocable key with appropriate spending limits, and revoke it through OpenAI if the device or key may be compromised.

  • You can deny camera or location permission, but the corresponding capture or routing feature will not work.
  • You can choose whether to enable drive recording, Debug retention, or saved analysis frames in Settings.
  • You decide whether to edit, share, export, or send any draft or dataset.
  • Review every AI verdict, recipient, and probable contract match before acting on it.

Children, changes, and contact

Pothole Reporter is a road-reporting tool and is not designed for children. It does not create user profiles or knowingly operate a child-directed service.

If this policy or the app's data flows change, this page will be updated and its effective date will change. Material new collection should be disclosed in the app before it begins.

For a privacy question or request, open a GitHub issue. GitHub issues are public: do not post an API key, precise location, unredacted road photo, or other sensitive information. You can describe the issue without attaching private data.

Pothole Reporter is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, OpenStreetMap, KSRSAC, or any government body.
