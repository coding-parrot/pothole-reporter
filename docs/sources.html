Provenance is not proof

The app combines a user's camera and phone location with public reference data, local rules, and AI judgments. A cited record does not prove that a photographed defect is reportable, that a particular authority owns the road, or that a contractor or warranty applies.

Review the photo, recipient, wording, and any contract candidate before sending a complaint.

Classification and complaint output

Pothole detection is a strict binary decision: Yes or No. The app does not show a detection-confidence percentage. A Yes is limited to a paved drivable surface with a localized cavity, broken edge or rim, and visible depth or material loss; an ambiguous view, unpaved damage, or speed breaker is No.

Each result records a surface type—bituminous/asphalt, cement concrete, mastic asphalt, paver blocks, unpaved/non-road, or unknown—and a strict defect type. For an accepted pothole, small/medium/large is an app visual estimate only. Length, width, and depth remain unknown unless they were field-measured; the report records measurement provenance and confidence so a visual estimate cannot be mistaken for an official municipal measurement or size class.

Authority-specific templates prepare a concise email, a short WhatsApp version, and copyable portal fields. Coordinates, map link, intake routing, road-owner status, and a contract block are mandatory in every format. The official tender reference, work title, organisation, status and dates, agreement/package/highway details, contractor when actually published, match basis, and source/detail links are preserved when available; missing facts are shown as unknown rather than inferred.

Versioned data packs

Large routing, contact, highway, and procurement datasets are not embedded in the APK. The v1.35 state-pack manifest pins 42 routing/contact/Karnataka resources, and the highway manifest pins 101 National Highway geometry tiles. Separate v1.36 catalogs pin 33 State/UT National Highway project packs, 36 PMGSY State/UT road-agreement packs, and 34 official State/UT road-notice packs. Every resource records its HTTPS URL, decoded byte length, and SHA-256 checksum; old catalogs remain immutable for existing clients.

The app downloads only a relevant pack from this project's GitHub Pages site, verifies the complete response before parsing it, and caches only verified bytes locally. Missing, malformed, truncated, or checksum-mismatched optional contract data never blocks a report; it continues without a candidate. Required routing/contact failures still fail closed. Delete all app data clears the pack cache.

The request contains no report, road photo, or exact coordinates. GitHub Pages can receive the device's IP address, standard connection metadata, and the requested URL; the URL reveals the selected state or an approximate 2° highway tile. The three catalog manifests add about 100 KB to a future build; the remote packs do not enter the APK, and the already-submitted Play closed-test binary is unchanged.

National Highways across India

The app checks National Highway geometry before any municipal route. It uses every operational NH/NE carriageway reference found in the pinned Geofabrik OpenStreetMap India extract dated 20 August 2026 (source MD5 c5e0a62a1cb00c80d8c5948bf18370d7), redistributed under the ODbL. The build accepted 142,656 source features spanning 680 distinct mapped references and published them as 101 immutable 2° tiles. Only the tile containing the reported point is requested, verified, and matched locally.

A geometric match suggests Rajmargyatra, CPGRAMS, or helpline 1033. It deliberately does not claim that NHAI maintains the road: National Highways can be maintained through NHAI, NHIDCL, BRO, or a State PWD. The user must verify the maintaining agency in the official service.

This is nationwide mapped coverage, not a legal inventory. Missing or incorrect OpenStreetMap references remain gaps. The matcher refuses GPS accuracy worse than 30 m, different nearby highway references, direction conflicts from a moving phone, missing tiles, altered checksums, and malformed data rather than falling through to a city recipient.

Optional contract context uses the official NHAI/MoRTH Data Lake current-project inventory and current NHIDCL notices. The 26 August scan read 3,258 MoRTH and 529 NHIDCL rows, normalized 1,950 current/open records (1,939 + 11), and published the 1,859 with a mapped NH/NE reference in 33 packs (1,805,723 bytes). A matching highway number, State/UT, and independent road/location evidence produce a candidate, not proof that the GPS point lies inside the package chainage. Open notices assert no award or contractor.

State and Union Territory road candidates

The official PMGSY scan read 211,007 rows and retained 17,717 recent records whose source status says In Progress. They occupy 36 State/UT packs (3,075,718 bytes); CH, DH, DL, GA, and LD currently have zero retained records. An agreement number/date verifies only that the source reports an agreement. This layer never claims an award, contractor, exact segment, warranty, or DLP.

The official notice catalog combines 30 zero-failure GePNIC feeds with dedicated Bihar eProc2, Chhattisgarh CHiPS, Gujarat nProcure, Telangana eProcurement, and Lakshadweep adapters. The 35 feeds scanned 69,754 rows, rejected 64,358 through strict scope/current-record checks, and published 5,396 notices in 34 packs (4,296,746 bytes). Exact IDs/references, titles, organisations where published, dates, and official sources are preserved; missing facts stay null. Andhra Pradesh remains blocked because its complete table requires undocumented client-encrypted state and exposes no stable record link. KPPP remains a separate Karnataka supplement.

A candidate is considered only in the same jurisdiction and only with independent road/immediate-locality evidence. Drain-, footpath-, sewer-, utility-, consultancy-, and roadside-plantation-only work is rejected. Runtime attempts National Highway, Karnataka KPPP, PMGSY, and official State/UT notice layers nationwide, but does not guarantee every public tender or road. Open notices are not awards. Exact segment, work scope, award, contractor, warranty, and DLP require authoritative evidence.

Authority profiles and ownership

Specific intake profiles cover BMC and the Bengaluru Central, East, North, South, and West city corporations. How to audit it

  1. Use the photo and printed coordinates to check the event itself.
  2. For every supported region, verify the suggested route and road owner in the official service and keep any grievance/reference ID it returns.
  3. For Karnataka, follow the address and GIS sources above to check location and routing.
  4. Use the cited official portal root and record identifiers to verify every candidate's exact segment, carriageway scope, award, contractor, and DLP terms. For Karnataka KPPP rows, search KPPP by tender number. Never infer DLP from publication date.
  5. Inspect the open-source build scripts and detailed provenance note for filtering and indexing logic.

Found stale or incorrect source data? Open a GitHub issue with a public source link. Do not include an API key, private personal data, or an unredacted road photo.
