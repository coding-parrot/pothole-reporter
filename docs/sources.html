Provenance is not proof

The app combines a user's camera and phone location with public reference data, local rules, and AI judgments. A cited record does not prove that a photographed defect is reportable, that a particular authority owns the road, or that a contractor or warranty applies.

Review the photo, recipient, wording, and any probable contract match before sending a complaint.

Versioned state packs

Large routing, contact, and procurement datasets are not embedded in the APK. The installed manifest currently pins eight resources by version, HTTPS URL, byte length, and SHA-256 checksum: routing/contact packs for Delhi, Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, and West Bengal, plus a separate optional Karnataka tender pack. The Gujarat pack contains routing rules and a relevance envelope, not an AMC polygon.

The app downloads a pack from this project's GitHub Pages site, verifies the complete response before parsing it, and caches only verified bytes locally. Missing, malformed, truncated, or checksum-mismatched required routing/contact data makes the affected route fail closed. Polygon routes never fall back to a geocoder place name; Ahmedabad is the documented exception and requires an exact structured city-or-municipality result as part of its rule. Failure of the optional Karnataka tender pack does not block a report—it continues without contract context. A verified cached pack can be read after its first successful download. On a subsequent pack use, caches past their unused limits are pruned automatically; Delete all app data clears the entire cache immediately.

The request contains no report, road photo, or exact coordinates. GitHub Pages can receive the device's IP address, standard connection metadata, and the requested URL; because the URL names a state, it reveals that coarse state. Keeping state data separate reduces the core APK and lets another state be added without embedding every state's data in every installation.

Maharashtra, Kolkata, and Delhi coverage

Pothole Reporter prepares evidence but does not log in, bypass OTP, call a complaint-write API, press Send, or read complaint status. Opening an app, portal, email draft, WhatsApp, Share, or Call is not a submission. An app/portal report can be marked submitted only after the user records the grievance/reference ID returned by the official service.

Mumbai Metropolitan Region

Coverage targets MMRDA's current official MMR extent and its 2019 extension notification: 9 municipal corporations, 9 municipal councils, Khalapur Nagar Panchayat, and the remaining rural MMR. Rural or ambiguous locations use the neutral Aaple Sarkar fallback instead of the nearest city.

The downloaded Maharashtra routing pack contains an outline made from OpenStreetMap relation 13312356 plus the Palghar, Vasai, Alibag, Pen, Panvel, and Khalapur taluka relations named by the notification. It is approximate: it measures about 6,264.9 km² versus MMRDA's published 6,328 km², and its whole-taluka polygons do not subtract the Scheduled Areas excluded by the 2019 notification. It is not a legal MMR boundary. Valid administrative polygons can suggest 11 bodies: BMC, Thane, Kalyan-Dombivli, Navi Mumbai, Ulhasnagar, Bhiwandi-Nizampur, Vasai-Virar, Mira-Bhayandar, Panvel, Ambarnath, and Kulgaon-Badlapur. The other eight bodies have no usable civic polygon in the source snapshot, so they, rural points, and overlaps use Aaple Sarkar. A Nominatim place name never selects the recipient. These are local routing aids, not proof of road ownership.

Pune Municipal Corporation

Pune coverage uses the PMC_Boundary layer published by the official PMC GIS through its GeoServer WMS and corresponding WFS GeoJSON layer. A copy is checked locally from the verified Maharashtra routing pack. It covers current Pune Municipal Corporation limits only and deliberately excludes Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC).

Kolkata Municipal Corporation

Kolkata coverage uses the SMARTCITY:wb_municipal_boundary feature published by the Government of West Bengal Urban Development & Municipal Affairs Department through its official Nagar GIS WFS. The feature was retrieved on 21 August 2026 and has ULB_Code 250299 and MUN_ID 250299_0000001. One source self-intersection was repaired with GDAL ogr2ogr -makevalid; the RFC 7946 result was written at seven-decimal precision, validated, and not topologically simplified. The runtime pins the repaired geometry SHA-256 fa9e157d8cdc8d918dd934a77a5dcde375d3108598412cb8ca3e19ca2d916bf5 and fails closed if it changes without a reviewed release. The WFS publishes no explicit reuse licence. Coverage is current KMC limits only, excluding Howrah, Bidhannagar/Salt Lake, New Town, and other neighbouring civic bodies. The polygon does not prove road ownership.

National Capital Territory of Delhi

Delhi coverage uses OpenStreetMap relation 1942586 under the ODbL, checked against the official Delhi State GIS scope. The 21 August 2026 routing data measures 1,483.885 km² and the runtime pins geometry SHA-256 3462ba68bdbbc1fdebc99403aa9e1f9db5e0b78e30ca138b2d25df7463506ab3. It covers the full Delhi NCT and excludes the wider NCR, including Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad, and Faridabad. The polygon decides coverage only; it cannot establish whether PWD, MCD, NDMC, Cantonment, DDA, NHAI, or another agency maintains a road.

External channels

The offered service depends on the suggested route: an official app or portal, a published municipal email address, or Aaple Sarkar. PMC uses PMC Road Mitra with PMC CARE as an alternate. Greater Mumbai can use BMC Pothole QuickFix, MyBMC WhatsApp at +91 89992 28999, or 1916. Kolkata uses KMC Grievance 2.0, with the official KMC app as an alternate, WhatsApp at +91 83359 88888, and helpline 1800 345 3375. Delhi uses PWD Sewa as a cross-agency road-grievance handoff, with Delhi PGMS as an alternate, WhatsApp at +91 81301 88222, and helpline 1908. The user must review and complete the complaint in the external service.

Contract matching is disabled throughout Maharashtra, Kolkata, and Delhi because the app has no authoritative, road-linked award and defect-liability feed for those routes.

Chennai, Hyderabad, and Ahmedabad

Greater Chennai Corporation

The Tamil Nadu pack contains OpenStreetMap relation 1766358, retrieved through Nominatim on 21 August 2026 and redistributed under the ODbL. The local polygon measures about 433.098 km². It was reviewed against GCC's official 2025 EDP_zoneBoundary_2025 layer. That service publishes no explicit reuse licence, so its geometry is validation evidence only and is not redistributed.

Coverage is GCC only, not the wider Chennai Metropolitan Area or adjacent urban bodies. The app offers GCC Public Grievance, Android package com.ceedeev.grivenancev2, WhatsApp at +91 94450 61913, and helpline 1913.

Partial Hyderabad core

The Telangana pack contains a conservative local polygon from OpenStreetMap relation 7868535, retrieved through Nominatim on 21 August 2026 under the ODbL. It is partial Hyderabad-core coverage, not a current CURE or corporation boundary. Telangana's G.O.Ms.No.55, linked from the official government-order register, created Greater Hyderabad, Cyberabad, and Malkajgiri as three corporations in February 2026; the app does not assign an accepted point to one of them. It offers the shared My Cure grievance service and its web flow.

The OSM outline overlaps Secunderabad Cantonment. Before polygon routing, the app refuses the complete published extent of TGRAC's official Cantonment Boundary layer. Only that four-number extent is stored; the unlicensed government polygon is not redistributed. This fail-closed rule also excludes some neighbouring civic points rather than risking a false My Cure route.

Ahmedabad structured match

The Gujarat pack contains no AMC polygon. A point must be inside the local relevance rectangle, 72.4200568–72.7400568 E and 22.8615374–23.1815374 N, and Nominatim must return an exact accepted Ahmedabad or Amdavad value in the structured city or municipality field with Gujarat as the state. District, postcode, free-form display text, and nearby names do not qualify. The reviewed identity is OpenStreetMap node 245711197, retrieved by a structured Nominatim search on 21 August 2026 under the ODbL. This is a relevance rule, not municipal containment or road ownership.

No current reusable AMC polygon was found. AMC's public AMC_Boundary layer was older than AMC's published current extent, had no explicit reuse licence, and had invalid HTTPS during review, so it is not bundled. An accepted match offers the official AMC CCRS, Android package com.amplvb.ccrs, WhatsApp at +91 75678 55303, and helpline 155303. AMC also publishes ccrs@ahmedabadcity.gov.in; this app does not currently offer that email handoff.

These routes prepare editable evidence only. Their coverage checks and handoffs do not prove road ownership, and contract matching is disabled.

Town boundary, gram-panchayat boundary, and national-highway check Karnataka GIS, operated by KSRSAC, is queried at the reported point. A boundary lookup does not by itself establish maintenance responsibility. If the required road-class or jurisdiction lookup fails, the app is designed to refuse municipal routing.
Municipal recipient The verified Karnataka routing/contact pack contains 182 supported local bodies. Addresses were compiled from Karnataka district NIC pages and local-body pages; entries are keyed by Local Government Directory town code. Contact details are a dated snapshot and may change. Bodies without a published address are deliberately unroutable.
Tender number, work title, publication date, and location The full source snapshot contains 42,283 road-related awarded works from the Karnataka Public Procurement Portal (KPPP). For eligible Karnataka routes only, the optional downloadable pack contains the 13,577 rows indexed to a supported municipal body. The title-keyword filter is intentionally broad. A tender row is not proof that its work covers the photographed point. Routes outside eligible Karnataka coverage never use contract matching.
Winning bidder on some Bengaluru records The full source snapshot has 1,124 contractor names derived from bengaluru-road-contracts.pages.dev, whose stated source is KPPP. The current downloadable pack retains 1,121. This is an intermediary snapshot rather than a fresh per-tender lookup. Most tender-pack rows have no bidder name, and the app must not invent one.
Probable contract match For an eligible Karnataka route, a deterministic local ranking selects up to 25 contracts indexed to the same supported body; an OpenAI model may then select one above the app's confidence threshold. The match is generated and probabilistic. No route outside eligible Karnataka coverage enters this flow.
Possible defect-liability or maintenance wording For an eligible Karnataka match, a local rule uses the tender's publication date: up to one year is described as possibly within a defect-liability period and up to three years as possibly within maintenance. The award-search row contains no actual warranty term. This is an inference and must never be presented as a contractual fact. It is generated only for an eligible Karnataka match.
Complaint text and signature A local template combines the reviewed result, coordinates, supported recipient, optional probable contract reference, and the sender name entered in Settings. The user must check and edit the draft. Karnataka opens an email composer; other supported routes offer a recorded official handoff. No path submits automatically.

Current coverage and data limits

  • MMR coverage targets all 19 urban local bodies and the remaining rural extent. The outer OSM outline is approximate and not a legal boundary. Eleven valid civic polygons can select a specific body; the rest use Aaple Sarkar. No suggestion determines road ownership.
  • Pune coverage is limited to the current official PMC GIS boundary. PCMC is outside coverage.
  • Kolkata coverage is limited to the current official KMC feature. Howrah, Bidhannagar/Salt Lake, New Town, and other neighbouring civic bodies are outside coverage.
  • Delhi coverage is the full NCT only. The wider NCR is outside coverage, and the NCT boundary does not establish road ownership.
  • Chennai coverage is GCC only. Its ODbL polygon was validated against, but is not copied from, GCC's official 2025 layer.
  • Hyderabad coverage is a conservative, partial ODbL core outline. Shared My Cure does not assign one of the three 2026 corporations. The complete official Secunderabad Cantonment layer extent is refused, so some neighbouring civic points are also excluded.
  • Ahmedabad depends on Nominatim and an exact structured city-or-municipality match inside a local envelope. It is not an AMC boundary and does not cover the wider AUDA area.
  • The project has no complaint-write API, status sync, or cross-user report database. Evidence stays local until the user deliberately opens an external handoff.
  • Contract matching is disabled for every route outside eligible Karnataka coverage.
  • The app covers supported Karnataka urban local bodies with a published recipient address; it does not claim statewide routing for every road.
  • Of 319 urban local bodies in the roster used to build the Karnataka routing/contact pack, 182 have a supported directory entry and 137 do not.
  • Of 42,283 road-related source rows, 18,972 are DMA or legacy BBMP records. The downloadable pack retains the 13,577 indexed to a supported body that can enter a contract shortlist.
  • The full source has 41,159 records without a winning bidder. The 13,577-row downloadable pack has 12,456 without one and 1,121 with one.
  • For Karnataka, national highways, rural roads, unknown road classes, and unsupported bodies are not routed to a municipal recipient. Other locations outside the stated MMR, PMC, KMC, Delhi NCT, GCC Chennai, Hyderabad-core, and Ahmedabad rules are not routed.
  • All counts describe the repository snapshot above, not the current contents of an external portal.
  • The public Nominatim endpoint is cached on an approximately 11 m grid and requests are serialized below one per second. A large deployment needs a policy-compliant managed or self-hosted service. MMR, PMC, KMC, Delhi, Chennai, and Hyderabad polygon routing remains local; Ahmedabad intentionally depends on the geocoder response.

How to audit it

  1. Use the photo and printed coordinates to check the event itself.
  2. For every supported region, verify the suggested route and road owner in the official service and keep any grievance/reference ID it returns.
  3. For Karnataka, follow the address and GIS sources above to check location and routing.
  4. Search KPPP by tender number and read the tender documents before relying on a probable match, bidder name, or warranty statement.
  5. Inspect the open-source build scripts and detailed provenance note for filtering and indexing logic.

Found stale or incorrect source data? Open a GitHub issue with a public source link. Do not include an API key, private personal data, or an unredacted road photo.
